What's inside

Four ingredients. That's the whole list.

Every Bowowowz biscuit is mixed, rolled, cut, and baked by hand at home. No fillers, no dyes, nothing you can't pronounce. Just the good stuff, baked until it's crunchy the way dogs like it.

Wheat flour Peanut butter Eggs Water

The icing on the specialty biscuits is made with four ingredients.

Peanut butter Plain Greek yogurt Blueberries Cornstarch
